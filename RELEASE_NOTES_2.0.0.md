# Quart17 2.0.0

## 中文

- 重新设计“锁屏列表大小”和“播放器圆角”滑块，修复标题与滑杆重叠，显示清晰的当前百分比；松手时才保存，拖动更顺畅。
- 锁屏通知列表等比例缩小通知卡片、间距和分组标题，隐藏位置错误的滚动指示器。
- 右半屏连续两次下滑清除普通通知，保留音乐控件和实时活动；清除成功时可选择轻震动。左半屏下滑不触发搜索。
- 整理设置页：清除手势和震动开关现在控制对应功能，移除失效的测试通知入口，补齐中英文说明。
- 延续 Quart 风格播放器、三种可拖动的进度样式、封面配色以及可替换按钮图标。

## English

- Redesigned the Lock Screen list size and player corner sliders. Their titles and tracks no longer overlap, each shows its current percentage, and changes are saved when dragging ends for smoother interaction.
- Notification list scaling now keeps cards, spacing, and group headings in proportion, and hides the misplaced scroll indicator.
- Two downward swipes on the right half of the Lock Screen clear ordinary notifications while keeping media controls and Live Activities. An optional light haptic confirms clearing. A downward swipe on the left half does not open Search.
- Aligned the gesture and haptic switches with their behavior, removed the nonfunctional test notification button, and updated Chinese and English settings text.
- Includes the Quart style player, three seekable progress styles, artwork colors, and replaceable button icons.
