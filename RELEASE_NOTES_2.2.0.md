# Quart17 2.2.0

## 中文

- 新增原生大封面入口：点击小封面以系统动画展开，点击大封面收起；点击歌名打开当前播放 App。
- 大封面新增独立的大小与圆角滑块，以及位于封面外侧、跟随封面颜色的环形播放进度条。
- 锁屏播放器新增 Quart 原风格与液态玻璃两种外观选择，并支持封面取色背景。
- 锁屏手电筒和相机按钮新增与通知相近的玻璃外观，保留原生图标和操作。
- 大封面展开时，播放器仍支持拖动调整播放进度。

## English

- Added a native expanded artwork entry: tap the compact cover to expand it with the system animation, tap the large cover to collapse it, or tap the song title to open the playing app.
- Added separate size and corner sliders for expanded artwork, plus an outer playback ring colored from the cover.
- Added a choice between the original Quart and Liquid Glass player appearances, with artwork-derived player backgrounds.
- Added a notification-matched glass appearance for the native flashlight and camera buttons while keeping their original icons and actions.
- Added player seeking while expanded artwork is visible.
